ATtiny13A LED Ladder Game — Wiring Diagram (CR2032 version)
============================================================

ATtiny13A Pinout (PDIP-8, top view)
------------------------------------

              ┌───┐
   (RESET) PB5│1 8│VCC ─── +3V (CR2032+)
         PB3 │2 7│PB2
         PB4 │3 6│PB1
         GND │4 5│PB0
              └───┘


Connections Summary
-------------------

  Pin   Function
  ───   ────────
  PB0   Charlieplex pin 0  (LED matrix)
  PB1   Charlieplex pin 1  (LED matrix)
  PB2   Charlieplex pin 2  (LED matrix)
  PB3   Charlieplex pin 3  (LED matrix)
  PB4   Button (to GND, internal pull-up, also wakes from sleep)
  PB5   RESET (leave unconnected or 10k to VCC)
  VCC   +3V (CR2032 positive terminal)
  GND   CR2032 negative terminal

LED Charlieplex Matrix
----------------------

Each LED connects between two pins. The ANODE (+, longer leg) goes to
the first pin, the CATHODE (-, shorter leg, flat side) goes to the second.

  LED#   Anode (+)   Cathode (-)    Position on ladder
  ────   ─────────   ───────────    ──────────────────
   0       PB0         PB1          Rung 1  (bottom)
   1       PB0         PB2          Rung 2
   2       PB0         PB3          Rung 3
   3       PB1         PB0          Rung 4
   4       PB1         PB2          Rung 5
   5       PB1         PB3          Rung 6
   6       PB2         PB0          Rung 7
   7       PB2         PB1          Rung 8
   8       PB2         PB3          Rung 9
   9       PB3         PB0          Rung 10
  10       PB3         PB1          Rung 11
  11       PB3         PB2          Rung 12 (top = GOAL)

Parts list
----------

  1× ATtiny13A (PDIP-8 or SOIC-8)
  12× LEDs — RED, GREEN, or YELLOW (low forward voltage ~1.8–2.2 V)
             Do NOT use blue or white (need ~3 V+, won't work on CR2032)
  1× 150 Ω resistor (1/4 W)
  1× 47 µF electrolytic capacitor (or 47 µF ceramic, 6.3 V+)
  1× tactile push button (6×6 mm)
  1× CR2032 coin cell + holder (20 mm, horizontal or vertical)
  1× 8-pin IC socket (optional, recommended)
  1× optional slide switch (for true 0 µA storage off)


CR2032-specific notes
----------------------

- LED COLOR MATTERS: Use red/green/yellow. Their forward voltage (~1.8–2.2 V)
  leaves headroom for the resistor at 3 V. Blue/white LEDs need ~3.0–3.4 V
  and will be extremely dim or not light at all.

- BULK CAPACITOR: The CR2032 has ~10–30 Ω internal resistance. The 47 µF
  capacitor supplies the short LED current pulses so the battery voltage
  doesn't sag. Place it close to the chip.

- SINGLE RESISTOR: 150 Ω limits peak LED current to ~5–8 mA at 3 V (depending
  on LED color). This is gentle enough for the coin cell. Only one LED is
  ever on at a time, so average current is much lower.

- BATTERY LIFE ESTIMATE:
    Active play:    ~2–5 mA average → ~40–100 hours of continuous play
    Standby (sleep): ~0.1 µA → years (limited by CR2032 self-discharge ~1%/yr)
  The auto-shutdown after 30 s of inactivity is the key power saver.

- OPTIONAL POWER SWITCH: Wire a small slide switch between CR2032+ and the
  VCC rail. This gives a true 0 µA off for long-term storage. Not needed
  for normal use — the firmware auto-sleeps.

- DECOUPLING: The 47 µF cap also serves as decoupling. No additional 100 nF
  needed (but you can add one for good measure).

- LED polarity: longer leg = anode (+), shorter leg / flat side = cathode (-).

- Button: PB4 to one side, GND to other side. No external resistor needed
  (internal pull-up is enabled in firmware).
